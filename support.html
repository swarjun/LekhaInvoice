Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account or the internet to use the app?

No. Lekha Invoice is offline-first. You can create invoices, manage inventory and track customers with no account and no internet connection — all your data is stored on your device.

What is Prime, and what does cloud sync do?

Prime is our optional paid plan. While it is active, your invoices sync through our secure cloud so you can access them across the devices on your account. Signing in alone does not upload anything — sync runs only while Prime is active. Only invoices dated on or after your Prime start date are synced; older invoices remain only on the device that created them.

How do I activate Prime?

To activate a Prime plan, contact us on WhatsApp (preferred) or by email using the details above, and we'll help you get set up.

What is the "master device"?

Each account has exactly one master device, which aggregates invoices from your other devices and owns the shared business profile. Your other devices upload their own invoices. You can change which device is the master, or take over as master if the original is lost, from Settings → Cloud & Sync.

How do I back up or export my invoices?

Cloud sync is a convenience, not a backup service. To keep your own copy, use the export option on the master device to save your invoices as an Excel or PDF file, and store it somewhere safe.

I lost my device / reinstalled the app. How do I get my data back?

If Prime is active and you sign in on the master device, your synced invoices will be restored from the cloud. Inventory and customer lists are stored locally and are not synced, so keep your own export if you need them back.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time from the master device — see our Account & Data Deletion page. If you no longer have access to your master device, email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I turn off ads?

Ads are shown only to users without an active Prime plan. Activating Prime removes ads.
